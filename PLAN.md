ROLE DESCRIPTION:

My job application tracker will essentially help with logging in your new applications, tracking their status (in the process, applied, online assessment stage, interview stage, awaiting status, offer!, rejected, withdrawn), and finally, generate small reminders for you to keep accountable! It will require your permission to change any saved data such as adding an application, marking any one as withdrawn, or deleting any record. 

REQUIRED INPUTS:

As a user, you will be required to give in the following inputs:
1) Target role type (e.g. AI Intern)
2) Target industry (e.g. "Healthcare", "Motorsport")
3) Application goal (e.g. 15 applications)
4) Deadline (e.g. YYYY-MM-DD)
5) Weekly availability (weekly hour commitment)

APPROVAL GATES:

Certain actions will always require confirmation before writing to the local file. I have mentioned some of these actions in the role description. An approval gate can look like: Add 'AI Summer 2027 Intern' as Applied? (Yes/No)
